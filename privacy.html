Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Ferroquant collects the following data when you use the Service:

  • Reddit username — provided by Reddit's authentication system
  • Votes and predictions — your bullish/bearish votes on trading signals
  • Streak and accuracy data — calculated from your prediction history
  • Premium status — whether you have purchased premium features

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Passwords or authentication tokens
  • Email addresses
  • IP addresses or device information
  • Browsing history outside the app
  • Financial or trading account information

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Display your predictions and accuracy on the leaderboard
  • Track your daily streak
  • Provide premium features if purchased
  • Resolve prediction outcomes and update scores

4. Data Storage

All user data is stored in Reddit's Devvit Redis infrastructure, which is isolated per-community installation. We do not export or transfer your data to external systems.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Your username and prediction accuracy are visible on the public leaderboard within the community where the app is installed.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as the app is installed in the community. If the app is uninstalled, all associated data in Devvit Redis is deleted by Reddit's platform.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • View your prediction history and accuracy within the app
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us via r/ferroquant_dev
  • Stop using the Service at any time

8. Third-Party Services

The Service operates within Reddit's platform and is subject to Reddit's Privacy Policy. Premium purchases are processed by Reddit's payment system.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy inquiries, reach out via r/ferroquant_dev.
